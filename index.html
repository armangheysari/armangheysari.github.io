About

A bit about me

Bridging systems research and applied AI to build resilient, intelligent infrastructure.

I'm a Mid-Level Data Scientist & AI Engineer with a Master's in Computer Engineering from Amirkabir University of Technology. My research sits at the intersection of fault-tolerant computing, distributed systems, and blockchain optimization.

During my MSc I designed genetic-algorithm-based optimization frameworks for Proof-of-Work blockchains and Byzantine fault-tolerant System-on-Chip architectures. I've also contributed to multi-objective optimization of Network-on-Chip designs and authored two patents on fault detection in safety-critical SoCs.

Beyond research, I enjoy mentoring students, building things from scratch, and exploring how reinforcement learning can make distributed systems more robust.
